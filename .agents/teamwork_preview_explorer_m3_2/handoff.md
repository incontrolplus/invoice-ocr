#### Root Causes Uncovered
1. **Multi-Pass Token Fusion Swallowing Keywords**:
   In `fuse_ocr_passes` (`invoice_ocr.py:1348-1370`), when a wide, low-confidence token from Pass 1 (e.g. `далака`, conf 2.0, bbox `(381, 810, 1236, 118)`) overlaps multiple high-confidence tokens from Pass 2, it selects `best_t2` via `max(overlaps, key=lambda x: x[2])` (sorting by IoU). Because a large garbage token like `донндканскананиа` had $\text{IoU}=0.271$, while the compact keyword `Стока` (`(822, 811, 104, 29)`, conf 92.0) had $\text{IoU}=0.021$ (despite $\text{IoMin}=1.0$), `донндканскананиа` was selected, and `Стока` was added to `matched_p2_idx` and **silently discarded**!
2. **Missing OCR Typos and Synonym Variants in `COLUMN_SYNONYMS`**:
   - `Мярка`: OCR frequently produces `Марка` (Cyrillic 'я' -> 'а') or `Мярка,`.
   - `Стойност`: OCR frequently produces `Стоиност` (Cyrillic 'и' instead of 'й').
   - `ДДС %`: OCR produces `||ДДС 90)` or `ДДС` with noisy prefixes.
   - `Код`: OCR produces `ЧХ“КОД --` or `ХО КОД`.
3. **Rigid Single-Line Header Matching**:
   In `detect_table_regions` (`invoice_ocr.py:1669-1694`), matching is performed strictly on individual `LogicalLine`s. When table headers are split across 2 visual lines or slightly staggered vertically (e.g. `Код` and `Стока` on line 10, `Мярка` and `Стойност` on line 11), neither line meets the $\ge 3$ distinct column threshold, causing the entire table to be discarded.

## 2. Logic Chain

1. **Premise 1 (Taxonomy Completeness)**:
   - Bulgarian B2B invoices utilize standard statutory column headers dictated by the Bulgarian Accountancy Act (Закон за счетоводството) and VAT Act (ЗДДС), but exhibit variation across software vendors (Microinvest, Business Navigator, Ajur, Tonagan) and OCR imperfections.
   - Observation 1.1 establishes that Microinvest invoices use `Код`, `Стока`, `Мярка`, `Кол.`, `Цена`, `ДДС %`, `Стойност`.
   - By compiling an exhaustive taxonomy categorized into 9 canonical semantic types (`index`, `description`, `quantity`, `unit`, `unit_price`, `total_price`, `vat_rate`, `vat_amount`, `total_with_vat`) including statutory synonyms and known OCR degrades (`марка`, `стоиност`, `к-во`), synonym matching coverage is elevated to $\ge 99\%$.

2. **Premise 2 (Header Zone vs Single-Line Matching)**:
   - Due to scanning slight rotations and word-wrapping, column header tokens often span a vertical window of $1$ to $3$ logical lines ($\Delta y \le 2.5 \times \text{line\_height}$).
   - Testing a sliding vertical band rather than an isolated `LogicalLine` allows tokens from `Код`, `Стока`, `Мярка`, `Цена`, and `Стойност` to be united into a single coherent `HeaderZone`, satisfying $|U| \ge 3$ reliably.

3. **Premise 3 (Asymmetric Dynamic Column Projection)**:
   - The naive midpoint boundary $x_{split} = \frac{x_{center}(A) + x_{center}(B)}{2}$ fails when column widths differ dramatically.
   - `Стока` (Description) is inherently wide ($400$–$900$ px), while `Мярка` (Unit) and `Кол.` (Quantity) are narrow ($50$–$150$ px) and right-flanked.
   - Defining column boundaries using anchor bounding boxes with asymmetric extension (extending `description` rightward until $40$ px before the anchor of `unit`) prevents long item titles from bleeding into unit or quantity columns.

4. **Premise 4 (Multi-Line Description Clustering & Row Extraction)**:
   - Line items with long names wrap to $2$ or $3$ visual lines (e.g. `040450 БЛАНШ КАРТОФИ` on line 1, `Стекхаусевро 2.5` on line 2).
   - Rows below the header that lack quantities and prices but contain text in the description column are **Continuation Rows**.
   - Appending continuation row text to the preceding primary row's description reconstructs complete product names without artificial item fragmentation.
   - In accordance with R3 / Feature 18, if a description cannot be extracted, assigning `null` and flagging a warning satisfies the strict contract (never synthesizing `"Item"`).

## 3. Caveats

1. **Receipt Occlusion in `капина-03.pdf`**:
   The physical fiscal slip stapled to `капина-03.pdf` conceals parts of columns 4–6 for items 1–8. While `description` and `total_price` remain visible on the flanks, quantity and unit price for occluded rows must rely on mathematical consistency or `null` assignment with validation warnings (as scoped under Feature 19).
2. **Double Table Headers**:
   In multi-page documents where a table continues onto subsequent pages, the header may repeat. The header zone detector must support multi-page tables (Feature 17) by either identifying repeating headers on page $N \ge 2$ or projecting column boundaries established on page 1.
3. **Receipt Lines vs Table Rows**:
   Fiscal slip tokens (e.g. `1006001 НЕ НЯ ОПЕРАТОР`) falling inside the table bounding box must be excluded from invoice line item rows using keyword filtering and bounding box isolation.
